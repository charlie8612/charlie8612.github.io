About

I infer hidden physical state from indirect, non-visual sensing, and use that inference to make robot manipulation robust under real-world uncertainty. My earlier work recovered hidden spatial state from Wi-Fi signals; my current work recovers hidden contact state from touch — the same problem, applied where it matters most for manipulation reliability.

At the Robot Learning Lab (advisor: Prof. Shao-Hua Sun), I led the real-world experiments for our CoRL 2026 submission on video-replanning-based implicit state estimation. My ongoing research asks whether the perception-level gains reported in tactile representation learning actually survive to the policy level — using unpaired onboarding of new tactile sensors as a controlled way to vary representations.
